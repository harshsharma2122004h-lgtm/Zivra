Clear answers.

Quick help for shopping, sizing, payments and delivery.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product, select available options, add it to your bag and complete the delivery form. Cash on Delivery is currently enabled.

How can I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter the order number and email used at checkout.

Can I create an account?

Yes. Use your mobile number and a password. OTP verification is not used when Phone Confirmations are disabled in Supabase.

Which payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery is currently available. Online payment is not active.

How do I choose my size?

Refer to the Size Guide and any product-specific measurements shown on the product page.

How do I request a return?

Contact support with your order number and reason. Please wait for return instructions before shipping an item.

How do I contact ZIVRA?

Use the Contact page to send a support request.
