Order Help

Everything you need before and after placing an order.

How do I place an order?

Browse the collection, open a product, choose the available size and quantity, add it to your bag and continue to checkout. Enter your delivery details and review the order before confirming.

How can I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter the order number and the email used at checkout. Your latest order status will appear when the order is available in the ZIVRA order system.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact ZIVRA as soon as possible with your order number. Changes or cancellation may not be possible once the order has been packed or shipped.

What payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery is currently the available checkout method. Any additional payment options will be shown at checkout when enabled.

Where can I find my orders?

Use My Account to sign in, or open Track Order and enter your order details.
